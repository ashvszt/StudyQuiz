About StudyQuiz

What is StudyQuiz?

● StudyQuiz is a free student project designed to help students turn their own reviewers into interactive quiz.

● StudyQuiz has built in AI named Libris. Libris is an AI assistant that helps student create quizzes from their study reviewer.

● StudyQuiz is a tool that helps students review their assigned reviewers

Purpose:

● The purpose of StudyQuiz is to make studying more engaging and effective by turning review materials into interactive quiz.

● the first thing you do is to review. once you have reviewed the subject you can submit the pdf or notes to StudyQuiz to generate you a possible question to your upcoming quizes.

Creator

● StudyQuiz was created by AshDev, a student from Cavite State University Tanza Campus, Section/Year BSIT 1-8.

Possible Issue

● Studyquiz still have a malfunction when creating quizes because Libris can't generate properly and then leave a blank choices because Libris is not a highly advanced AI.

Privacy

● Your uploaded study files are processed only to generate your quiz. We don't expose uploaded files publicly, and one user can never access another user's files. Temporary uploaded files are deleted after processing whenever possible, and we don't store sensitive personal information unnecessarily.
